## Approach changes (and why)
- **5b(b) holds the loaded `ready` pose, not rest (D8, user).** At rest the joints sit on their stops, so a torque-on hold there would repeat (a). (a) and (b) are each judged against their own predicted ±1σ, not subtracted, because their geometry differs. (b) runs as a tour file for the existing `pose_tour.py`, not a new script: it reuses the reviewed planner and safety, and parks before torque-off.
- **The 5b(b) sag follow-up moved to step 11 (D9, user).** In 30 min, time and temperature rise together, so a longer hold, imaging through the cool-down and a return-to-pose test are needed to tell the causes apart.
- **Rule: nobody touches the table during a run.** The camera's tripod shares the table.
- **The iPad is protected by a pan range, not a keep-out box (D10, user).** The collision model only knows the table. The iPad couldn't move out of reach without losing half the board, though half a board would still be ~10× more precise than the tag.
- **The step 6 extremes are taught by hand, unpowered (6a, D10, user).** This checks focus and visibility before any motion, and the taught poses define the experiment's range.
- **Soft focus is usable:** the "usable" threshold went from 0.18 to 0.30 px edge RMS after the blur test. Sharp poses are still preferred for absolute accuracy (step 10).
- **Volume = a spherical sector** in r / θ / φ (user's idea; θ ≈ pan, r ≈ reach). It's filled with an even Cartesian grid, and every point is filtered: z ≥ 30 mm, the taught pan range, predicted visibility, and a table check on every move.
- **No fixed gripper orientation.** A 45°-down tool angle reached 1 point. Position-only IK seeded from taught poses, picking the wrist angle that turns the tag most squarely to the camera, reaches 28.
- **Visibility is predicted with a hand-eye fit**, not linear fits. The linear fits of distance and image position (±10 mm, ±150 px) couldn't see orientation.
- **Every sector tour starts at `ready`.** It's a known table-safe first move off the rest stops, and gives a repeated sharp reference shot.

## Surprises
- **The background-shift sign was inverted** (since 10-28). `cv2.phaseCorrelate(a, b)` already returns the content's motion from a to b. Caught because the iPad and tag PnP poses moved together while "bg" said the opposite. 10-29's "rel dy −0.51 px" was really ≈ −0.09 px. LESSONS #5.
- **Touching the table moved the camera ~0.13 mm in 30 min,** and put a +17 µm step after the first shot. Hands off: camera ≤ 30 µm, no step. The relative pose cancelled the camera motion but not the step.
- **The 5b(b) sag is invisible to the servos:** +258 µm in 30 min while every joint read constant (0 ticks peak-to-peak). One elbow tick ≈ 230–380 µm at the tag. The sag tracks time better than temperature (line residual 12 µm vs 20 µm). The elbow went 30 → 39 °C and levelled off; the sag didn't.
- **Depth of field set the workspace,** not the iPad. The camera looks back at the arm along a diagonal, about 30° off the arm's x axis: camera distance ≈ 817 − 0.70x + 0.42y + 0.19z mm. So "same x" doesn't mean "same distance", which explained the sharpness pattern in the teach map that confused the user.
- **teach_poses crash (`…140457`):** I wrote `teach.json` into the raw dir of a session that was still running. Its end-of-session write then hit the raw-immutability guard. All 15 poses survived in the manifest. Results now go to `data/derived/`.
- **SimBus caught a real hazard:** a direct move from rest to p00 came within 2.9 mm of the table, and SafeArm froze as designed. My first sim run looked like a hang for 10 min because I piped it through `tail`.
- **The wrist turned the tag away** at 5 of 23 points in the first dry run (user saw it). The hand-eye fit then predicted all 5 from joint angles alone: tilt 87–88° (edge-on), or above the frame at 78–84°.
- **The camera's field of view now limits the top of the workspace.** At x 290–370, z 230–300, wrist angles that face the tag at the camera put it above the top edge (row −100 to −700), and in-frame angles turn it away.
- **Gravity droop past a limit:** shoulder_lift read 3164 at p07, 6 ticks past its 3158 software limit.

## Learnings
- Blur from defocus mostly adds noise, not bias, if it's the same on both sides of an edge. A single image predicts the cost from blur width and noise ÷ edge steepness: sharp 3–3.5 px / 0.012 px, soft 8–10.6 px / 0.032–0.041 px.
- Pose error from one tag is a cigar along the line of sight. In 5b(a), dx/dz ≈ −0.15, matching the tag's off-axis angle (−0.14): depth noise leaking into camera x, plus tilt–depth correlation, not motion.
- A joint-space box built from taught extremes is not a safe or visible workspace. Its corners combine extremes never taught. Pan bounds do carry over (a convex region that excludes the base keeps its angular range).
- The hand-eye residual (27 px ≈ 2.2 mm at the tag) is the nominal kinematic model's error, not detection noise. Fine for planning; step 10 fits the model.
- `cv2.Rodrigues` rounds rotations below ~1e-5 rad to zero (seen 10-29); use scipy for small deltas in Jacobians.

## Decisions (+why)
- **Step 7 (ring-down) deferred (D11, user):** this camera can't characterise dynamics. A 1/30 s exposure averages a few-Hz wobble, a capture takes ~6 s (one sample per move), and the trigger lag is unmeasured. It can only tell whether a pose is settled. Settle stays 3 s (52 dry-run poses: sharp photos, no stillness flags). My one reservation: the stillness check is encoder-based and blind to µm link motion, so step 8 takes a second shot ~10 s after the first at each pose as a cheap settle check. Write-up: a stated limitation. Dynamics would need a fast sensor (e.g. phone slow-motion video at 240 fps, or an accelerometer), not this rig.
- Accept the field-of-view limit for now; re-aim or raise the camera only if a later step needs that height (user).
- Run the 28-point plan as is and decide coverage from what's lost (user); it lost only p05, a borderline point.
- Keep the iPad where it is (half the board visible); the pan range keeps the arm off it.
