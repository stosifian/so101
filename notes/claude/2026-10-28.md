## Surprises
- Capture takes 5.9 s per 67.6 MB RAW (spec assumed 1–3 s): ~180 poses/h instead of 240.
- A flat iPad viewed at ~70° off-axis is both a bad calibration geometry and much dimmer (LCD viewing angle); propping it up fixed both.

## Numbers
- Exposure path: f/11 1/60 ISO 800 → iPad whites 4 %; 1/30 ISO 800 (flat iPad) → 6 %; 1/30 ISO 1600 (propped) → 78–82 %, unclipped.
- Glare: clean black squares ~1 % of full scale, with window/blind reflections 6–13 %.
- **Image stability (`20261028-143956_stability_camera`, 3 shots 5 s apart, static iPad 9×6):** shift ≤ 0.07 px (≈ 5 µm at the iPad), residual 0.03 px RMS (max 0.07): corner noise ≈ 0.02 px per image. Rig + detection are far below the 0.1–0.2 px calibration target.
- **12-shot stability (`20261028-150406_stability_camera`, ~6 s apart, ~70 s):** shift sd 0.024 px in x, 0.055 px in y (max |shift| 0.12 px ≈ 9 µm at the iPad), dy trend ≈ +0.08 px/min (≈ 6 µm/min; too short to call drift); residual 0.03–0.04 px RMS, max 0.11. Shift jitter is ~10× what corner noise alone gives for a 54-corner mean (~0.005 px), so it is real motion of a few µm, larger vertically (iPad on its stand, tripod or table). Negligible vs the 0.25 mm budget; step 5 measures it over hours.
- Body firmware 3.01 (gphoto2 `--summary` "Version"), gphoto2 2.5.32.

## Decisions (+why)
- iPad as world reference (user): flat rigid glass, no humidity change; caliper still needed for absolute scale (≤ 0.05 %).
- gphoto2 CLI per capture (not python-gphoto2): a hung camera is a timeout, not a frozen script.
