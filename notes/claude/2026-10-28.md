## Decisions (+why)
- iPad as world reference (user): flat rigid glass, no humidity change; caliper still needed for absolute scale (≤ 0.05 %).
- gphoto2 CLI per capture (not python-gphoto2): a hung camera is a timeout, not a frozen script.
