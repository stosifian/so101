## Learnings
- Re-anchoring a frame on poses where the model fits poorly moves the error into the frame: pick anchor poses where the model is good (or many diverse ones), or the anchor bias dominates later accuracy numbers.
- The encoder correction halves the landing scatter (2.1 → 1.3 mm 3D, 1.9 → 0.9 mm lateral): the deadband/droop part is fixable from the servos' own readings.
