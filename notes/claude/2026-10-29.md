## Learnings
- **OpenCV's corner refinements are biased for outer tag corners:** SUBPIX pulls each corner ~0.17 px inwards (≈ 0.2 % scale on a 180 px tag); APRILTAG returns pixel-corner coordinates (+0.5 px). Hence our own edge-line fit.
- **Gamma moves edges:** an edge fitted on a gamma-encoded image shifts towards the dark side, shrinking the tag by 0.2–0.6 px per edge depending on blur. Blur depends on focus and depth, so this would be a pose-dependent depth bias. Edge work must use linear RAW data. ChArUco saddle corners are symmetric and unaffected.
- The full 2×2 grid on a card would have overhung the plate by ~5 cm. That is ~70 µm of self-weight sag that changes with pose, a natural frequency of ~70 Hz, and creep, all comparable to the effects being measured. A small tag on solid plastic avoids all three.
- Paper-to-card: thin permanent double-sided tape over the full area. Avoid CA, PVA and glossy overlays.

- `cv2.Rodrigues` rounds rotations below ~1e-5 rad to zero, which silently zeroes finite-difference Jacobians; use scipy's `as_rotvec` for small deltas.
