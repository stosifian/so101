## What I learned / what clicked
- iCloud tracking apparently is a big issue (for any project, use dev for now)
- the arm would have lunged with a naive torque-on as at power-up every servo's goal is position 0 with unlimited speed (verify?). Need to read registers before writing anything (same lesson from shoggoth): always know your motor state before doing anything!
- Collisions can occurs with itself and table! Apparently should be simulated (few instances this is documented)
- You can create FW simulator that you can test the modules with to see what happens if there's a misread, delay, failed handshake, etc.
- stick slip can occur at slow motions, need to speed up (3x proved worthwhile)
